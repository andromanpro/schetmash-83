# Prompt: «Бюрократический оракул» v1

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: high-end landscape key concept for redesigning the existing browser game's 3D cabinet

Input image: `shots/play.png` is the structural reference and edit target. Preserve
the recognizable overall proportions and front-facing composition of the cabinet,
but transform its character and material storytelling.

Primary request: Turn the machine into “СЧЁТМАШ-83 — the bureaucratic oracle”,
an original sentient Soviet retrofuturist accounting apparatus: intimidating,
absurdly official, warm, worn, and strangely alive. It should feel like a machine
that has spent forty years approving quarterly reports and judging operators.

Subject/invariants: Keep exactly three genuinely cylindrical curved slot reels
behind one shared deep convex panoramic glass. Keep the red lever fully outside
the cabinet on the right, visibly separated by air from the cabinet silhouette,
with a heavy mechanical hub. Keep a real coin slot, one red physical button,
mechanical credit/bet/win counters, and a readable five-route payline display.
Do not flatten the reels and do not merge the lever into the housing.

Character: Make the upper section the machine's restrained “face” without literal
cartoon eyes: two mismatched indicator lamps, ventilation slits and a narrow
electromechanical message tape create a stern expression. The machine is slightly
asymmetrical, as if it has posture. A narrow paper receipt emerges from a slot
with a bold red approval stamp. Add an inventory plate, old inspection seal,
maintenance scratches, fingerprints around the coin slot, chipped dark-green
enamel, aged cream bakelite, oxidized restrained brass, grease around the lever
pivot, tiny relay glow and believable mechanical seams.

Reel art: bold varied caricature silhouettes rather than uniform medallions; one
visible Boris Nuraliev caricature is unmistakably the dominant golden symbol,
larger in visual weight than the others, with a mechanical halo and direct gaze.
Other 1C-themed signs are satirical documents, database creature, angry bug,
code scroll and red 1C tile. Preserve the circular curvature and natural
proportions of every image.

Scene/backdrop: no walls and no room. Infinite near-black charcoal stage, a
single industrial overhead spotlight, long grounded shadow, subtle haze and a few
dust motes. The floor is only a dim reflective patch beneath the machine and
disappears into black.

Composition: 16:9 landscape, complete machine visible with breathing room,
near-frontal view with a subtle low angle, strong iconic silhouette. Cabinet
centered, external lever fully visible. No web HUD or floating interface buttons
from the reference screenshot.

Lighting/mood: warm tungsten interior glow against cool green enamel, a little
ominous but humorous, cinematic contrast, practical lights only, no rainbow
casino neon. Quiet idle state that looks ready to wake up.

Text (verbatim, accurate Cyrillic): top nameplate “СЧЁТМАШ–83”; narrow message
tape “ДОКУМЕНТ ПРОВЕДЁН”; red stamp on receipt “ПРОВЕДЕНО”. No other large text.

Style/medium: polished cinematic industrial-design concept art, physically
believable materials, premium 3D keyshot with hand-authored wear, original Soviet
institutional sci-fi satire, not a direct copy of any existing franchise.

Avoid: clean generic CAD render, steampunk clutter, excessive brass borders,
slot-machine casino clichés, walls, room corners, humans, embedded or half-hidden
lever, rectangular flat reels, stretched illustrations, extra reels, giant
glowing tubes, cute googly eyes, illegible random lettering, logos, watermark.
